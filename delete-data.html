Delete your data

Triad: Daily Planner & Focus · Harshit Creative Studio · Last updated 23 September 2026

01

The short version

Almost everything Triad stores lives only on your phone, and you can erase it yourself in two taps. Only two things ever leave your device: an anonymous crash report if the app crashes, and — if you buy Triad Pro — a record of that purchase. Here is how to delete each of them.

02

Delete the data on your phone

  1. Open Triad and go to the Account tab.
  2. Tap Clear local data and confirm.

This permanently and immediately erases your tasks, daily plans, focus sessions, streak, settings, and the answers you gave during setup, including the first name you entered. Uninstalling Triad does the same. We never had a copy of any of it, so nothing is kept anywhere. If you exported a backup file, delete that file yourself.

03

Ask us to delete the data we hold

  1. Email harshitcreativestudio@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Triad data.
  2. If you bought Triad Pro, include your Google Play order number (it starts with GPA.). You'll find it in your Google Play receipt email, or in the Play Store under Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history. Triad has no accounts, so this number is the only way we can find your purchase record.
  3. We'll reply to confirm, and complete the deletion within 30 days.
04

What is deleted, and what is kept

Purchase record — deleted on request. Our purchase provider, RevenueCat, holds an anonymous app ID and your purchase or subscription status. We delete it when you ask. Your purchase itself stays with Google Play, so you can still restore Pro later; doing so creates a new record.

Crash reports — deleted automatically within 90 days. If the app crashes, a report goes to our error-reporting provider, Sentry: the error message, a stack trace, and your device model, OS version and app version. Reports contain no name, email or task text and aren't linked to you, so we can't pick out yours on request — they are deleted automatically within 90 days.

Payment details — kept by Google, not by us. Google Play holds your payment and order history under Google's own privacy policy. We never see your card details and can't delete Google's records. To cancel a subscription, use the Play Store: Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

Everything else — there is nothing else. We don't hold your tasks, your name, your email or any account, because Triad doesn't have one.
